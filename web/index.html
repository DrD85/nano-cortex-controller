Nano Cortex Controller

in the browser
Not connected

Connect

Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone. Click CONNECT and choose your Nano Cortex. The Nano takes one connection at a time: close the editor and switch off a controller board first.

Use it

The screen works like the touch screen: tap, hold, swipe. The eight footswitches below work like the real ones: click, or hold for 0.6 s for their held function. Keys 1–8 press them too. On a phone, turn it sideways for the screen alone (SCREEN does the same on a computer).

MIDI

The MIDI button on the screen lists the MIDI inputs of this computer (for example an MC6 over USB) and Bluetooth MIDI device for a Bluetooth controller such as an MC6 / MC8 Pro or a WIDI – also on the iPhone. Banks, symbols and FX presets are stored in this browser.
